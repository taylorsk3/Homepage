About Structure Project

The Structure project is a fun and interactive space designed from a purse. Building from concepts of how you thought adulthood was when you were a child. I used to carry a purse when I was young. I didn't really have anything to carry, so I filled the bag with rocks. I remember it made me feel like a real woman. This is a silly way of representing my purse, how it may vary on the day that I am having
